Yasko

Yasko is a personal content tool for the Turkish YouTube channel Yasko. It helps the channel owner prepare short vertical videos with original Turkish narration and commentary about films and TV series, and publish them to the owner's own channel.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos, captions and playlists to the owner's YouTube channel on a schedule.
  • Shows the owner a statistics page for the channel (views, subscribers, watch time).
  • Collects video requests from comments on the owner's own videos.

It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to the public.
