Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Yasko is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

With your permission, Yasko uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube Data API so it can upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and tags on your channel.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Yasko does not sell, share or send your Google data to any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Yasko's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke Yasko's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
